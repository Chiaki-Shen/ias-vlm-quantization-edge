## Results by Dataset

Each dataset is reported with its own metric. There is deliberately **no pooled TOTAL**: datasets differ in size and metric (MCQ accuracy vs TextVQA accuracy).

| Dataset | Metric | Done/Target | Answered | Errors | Retries | Unparsed | Score (raw) | Score (answered) | Avg tok/s | Time |
|---------|--------|-------------|----------|--------|---------|----------|-------------|------------------|-----------|------|
| scienceqa | accuracy | 2017/2017 | 2017 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 83.29% | 83.29% | 20.8 | 2011.5s |
| textvqa | TextVQA accuracy | 5000/5000 | 4995 | 5 | 119 | 0 | 74.74% | 74.82% | 20.6 | 15486.1s |

**Done/Target** counts records actually attempted; **pending** = never attempted.  
**Score (raw)** = score sum / records attempted (request errors count as 0)  
**Score (answered)** = score sum / answered (errors excluded) — use this for cross-config comparison  
**Unparsed** = answered records whose reply produced no usable prediction

*Secondary only:* unweighted macro-average of per-dataset answered scores = 79.06% over 2 complete datasets. It mixes MCQ accuracy and TextVQA accuracy; do not use it as a headline number.
