| blink_spatial | accuracy | 143/143 | 143 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 71.33% | 71.33% | 16.9 | 201.3s |
| cv_bench | accuracy | 2638/2638 | 2520 | 118 | 155 | 0 | 74.45% | 77.94% | 16.8 | 8662.2s |

**Done/Target** counts records actually attempted; **pending** = never attempted.  
**Score (raw)** = score sum / records attempted (request errors count as 0)  
**Score (answered)** = score sum / answered (errors excluded) — use this for cross-config comparison  
**Unparsed** = answered records whose reply produced no usable prediction

*Secondary only:* unweighted macro-average of per-dataset answered scores = 72.87% over 3 complete datasets. It mixes MCQ accuracy and TextVQA accuracy; do not use it as a headline number.
